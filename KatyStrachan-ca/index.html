Welcome to My Practice

I am a registered psychologist with a doctoral degree in Psychology from the University of British Columbia and I have been in private practice on the North Shore since 1994. My office is located in West Vancouver across from Park Royal at the end of Clyde Avenue.

I use a combination of therapeutic approaches such as Cognitive Behavioural therapy and mindfulness oriented therapy to help people create change in dealing with depression, anxiety and anger. If you are not familiar with psychotherapy, I would be happy to talk to you to help you decide if my approach would help address your concerns.
