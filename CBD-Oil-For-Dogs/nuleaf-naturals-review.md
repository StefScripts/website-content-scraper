# NuLeaf Naturals CBD for Dogs Review

## About the brand: Who is Nuleaf Naturals?

NuLeaf Naturals is a CBD company that’s committed to organic farming practices. They operate on a seed-to-shelf basis, meaning they oversee all parts of manufacturing to ensure quality.

NuLeaf Naturals produces an all-organic CBD oil with a good range of additional cannabinoids. Generally speaking, the wider the range of cannabinoid compounds found in a CBD product, the stronger its effects.

It’s clear that NuLeaf Naturals invests a lot of energy in sourcing and producing their CBD oil. They use best-farming and manufacturing practices, including a food-safe supercritical CO2 extraction method that helps to retain the most delicate compounds during processing.

Nuleaf Naturals Pet CBD Oil, derived from a full spectrum CBD extract from organic hemp grown in Colorado, demonstrates a robust cannabinoid profile. According to the latest lab report for their 900mg tincture, it contains 933mg of CBD and an additional 73mg of cannabinoids, including CBC, CBDa, CBDV, and THC.

This assortment of four extra cannabinoids surpasses the usual average of three found in CBD oils across a review of over 30 brands, and its 8% additional cannabinoid content aligns with the 8% market average.

The lab report, dated March 15th, 2023, ensures the product’s information is current, an important consideration for pet owners seeking reliable data on CBD products.

Nuleaf Naturals opts for a hemp seed oil as the carrier. While a more nutritious carrier oil than other options, we found several recent reviews complaining about a terrible taste and it being hard on their dog’s stomach, which is something to keep in mind. The oil is available in three practical sizes – 300mg, 900mg, and 1800mg – catering to different dosage needs and preferences.

#### **Verdict**

In terms of affordability, Nuleaf Naturals Pet CBD Oil stands out. With pricing at $0.05 per mg of CBD for the largest concentration and $0.06 for the smallest, it offers a slightly more economical option compared to the average market price.

While it may not rank as the highest echelon of CBD oils for dogs, it presents as a commendable choice for pet owners looking for a full spectrum CBD oil that balances quality and cost-effectiveness. Do keep in mind that it may not be for picky pups or dogs with sensitive stomachs.
