# Holistapet CBD Review

## About the brand: Who is Holistapet?

Holistapet is a pet-focused brand offering some of the most cannabinoid-rich broad spectrum CBD oils and treats available to dogs. While we prefer full spectrum CBD, Holistapet is our recommendation for those seeking a THC free option.

Holistapet does come up short in terms of consistency and customer service, however. While some of its lab reports show much higher levels of CBD than the advertised amount, others show significantly lower levels. Customers have also complained of long shipping times and unresponsive customer service.

Nonetheless, Holistapet is the only broad spectrum CBD for dogs that comes close to matching the high levels of beneficial cannabinoids as its full spectrum counterpart. They also offer a 30-day money back guarantee if you or your dog are not satisfied.
