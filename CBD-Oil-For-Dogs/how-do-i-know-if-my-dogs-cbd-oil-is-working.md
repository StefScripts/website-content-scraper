# How do I know if my dog’s CBD oil is working?

CBD oil can take time to build up in the body and start working, and its effects vary from dog to dog. A steady dosage over a consistent period, with some careful observation to see whether CBD is producing positive changes to your pup’s daily wellbeing. In this article we’ll talk about:

  * Go through some of the outward signs that may suggest your dog is feeling improvements with their symptoms thanks to CBD oil.
  * How CBD gets to work on your dog’s endocannabinoid system—a part of the body responsible for lowering inflammation and anxiety.



## Three signs that suggest CBD is doing its job

So how do you know when CBD is beginning to take effect on your dog’s endocannabinoid system? Here are three common signals that your pup is feeling a reduction in stress or inflammation:

__

### 1\. Increased movement, agility, and willingness to stand up

Inflammation and stress are both natural movement inhibitors—they are both attempts by the body to make us avoid further damage, and stay right where we are. An anxious dog may have a particular safe space, as well as areas or places that trigger panic. A dog with chronic inflammation might take some serious coaxing (and food bowl waving) to move at all. 

**If you’re noticing:**

  * A reduction in the time it takes for your pup to stand up,
  * An increase in bravery or willingness to explore,
  * Your dog expanding their garden patrol or spending time in different places in your home,
  * Less licking or fussing over joints and pressure points



Then it’s likely that CBD oil treatment is having a positive effect!

__

### 2\. Changes in verbalization

Dogs under stress from anxiety or inflammation in the body often present with verbalization changes. Depending on the dog in question, this might mean an increase in growling, snapping, and guard-dog responses. Other dogs may become more introverted, quiet, and passive. 

If CBD is reducing your pup’s inflammation or anxiety, it’s likely you’ll see this expressed in the way they communicate with the people and animals around them. Just as their endocannabinoid system should be becoming more stable, expect to see more stable expressions of behavior—so less panicking when the doorbell rings, or more acceptance of being petted and handled.

* * *

__

### 3\. Symptom reduction

If you’re giving your dog CBD oil to help with chronic conditions such as arthritis or seizures, the best way to judge effectiveness to compare symptoms before you begin CBD treatment, and after your pet has been ingesting the oil for a couple of weeks. Focus on things like frequency, duration, intensity, and how disruptive or upsetting symptoms are to your pet. 

In this sense, CBD may not completely remove your dog’s symptoms in the way that pharmaceutical drugs sometimes can. Instead, indications should become less severe over time, as internal cannabinoid concentrations rise. All dogs are different, and it may be necessary to adjust the type or dosage of CBD oil (within reason) until symptoms are manageable or completely gone.

### A note on CBD oil application:

It’s worth mentioning that oil applied directly on the gums or into the mouth is usually absorbed much faster than when eaten as a treat or in a bowl of food. So in the initial periods, you may want to experiment with this more immediate form of application.

On the other hand, ingesting CBD with food may turn out to be less hassle for both you and your dog over time.

### How does CBD actually work?

In humans and dogs alike, CBD (real name: cannabidiol) functions by boosting the body’s endocannabinoid system.

Endocannabinoids are signaling molecules that form an extensive network inside the body— connecting to all organs and systems. It’s thought that endocannabinoids are responsible for maintaining internal balance and harmony.

Cannabis products can both mimic endocannabinoids and boost natural levels of these transmitter molecules, which affect a sweeping set of behavioral, neuroendocrine, and autonomic functions. In other words, pain, inflammation & arthritis, memory, stress, sleep, and the immune system.

Among its endocannabinoid-enhancing effects, CBD has been found to bolster supplies of anandamide—a neurotransmitter that gets its name from the Sanskrit for ‘joy, bliss, or delight.’

Anandamide receptors are thought to be the primary target of THC (the cannabinoid in cannabis that produces those mind-altering effects, or highs). So, you might think of anandamide as one of the body’s natural mood boosters.

A failing endocannabinoid system is connected to a variety of disease states and chronic inflammatory conditions. For this reason, another benefit of supplementing with plant-derived cannabinoids is to ensure the body always has a steady supply of cannabinoids on tap. It’s this process that benefits most from a steady and consistent dosing of CBD oil.

Interestingly, cannabis isn’t the only source of plant-based cannabinoids. While CBD is the most highly concentrated source, carrots, cloves, black pepper, ginseng, and echinacea have all been found to produce their own varieties of these beneficial compounds.

## Summary

  * **CBD works by boosting the endocannabinoid system.** This system plays an important role in lowering stress and inflammation across the entire body. CBD is thought to mimic and/or boost naturally existing endocannabinoids.
  * **Don’t expect to see results right away.** While application to the gums may show a mild immediate effect, anecdotal evidence suggests that consistent dosages over a few weeks produce the best results. This should help the endocannabinoid system become saturated with cannabinoids.
  * **Look for increased movement** , a positive change in barking habits, or acute symptom reductions, as signs that your dog may be beginning to respond to the benefits of CBD oil. Trust your instincts—you know your pup better than anyone else does!
