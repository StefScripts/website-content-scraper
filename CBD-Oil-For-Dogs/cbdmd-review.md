# cbdMD Pet CBD Review

## About the brand: Who is cbdMD?

A contender for the biggest CBD brand around, cbdMD produces cannabis extract products for humans and pets. For dogs and cats, they operate under the brand name Paw CBD, so consider this page also relevant to Paw CBD reviews.

One of the most impressive aspects of cbdMD is their zero-THC commitment. THC is the psychoactive (high-causing) compound in cannabis, which can cause disorientation and trauma in dogs. While many CBD providers are satisfied to produce products with (safe) trace amounts of THC, the compound is undetectable in Paw CBD products, as proven by independent lab testing.

With such a large operation, cbdMD is able to oversee their entire production process, from hemp cultivation to the 60-day satisfaction guarantee. They only work with domestically sourced hemp and are the only pure-play CBD company ever listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

cbdMD also uses their human product range to sponsor athletes, allowing them to use CBD as part of their training program to speed up recovery and reduce muscle inflammation.
