# Spruce CBD for Dogs Review

## About the brand: Who is Spruce CBD?

Spruce is a new CBD company, having formed less than two years ago. While they didn’t make this year’s list of the [Best CBD Oil for Dogs](https://cbdoilfordogs.com/best-cbd-oil-for-dogs/), this North Carolina-based brand has a lot going for it. Here’s what we know about Spruce and their Full Spectrum Pet CBD.

Spruce was founded in 2018, by a family who has become disillusioned with modern medicine as it appears in both human and veterinary practice. While they don’t grow their own hemp, Spruce CBD is aligned with two farms, one in Kentucky and the other in North Carolina, which allows them to keep a close eye on their product from seed to shelf.

Spruce Dog CBD Oil 750mg, with its full-spectrum CBD extract sourced from non-organic hemp in Kentucky and North Carolina, presents an impressive cannabinoid profile in its recent lab report. The 750mg tincture contains 812mg of CBD and an additional 57mg of other beneficial cannabinoids, including CBC, CBDV, CBG, CBN, and THC.

This blend, featuring 5 extra cannabinoids, surpasses the average of 3 additional cannabinoids found in the oils from 30 brands reviewed. These make up an 8% additional cannabinoid content compared to the CBD content, which aligns with the industry average.

The product’s lab report, dated October 24, 2023, ensures current and reliable data on its composition, a significant factor for quality assurance.

Spruce employs MCT oil as its carrier, known for enhancing CBD absorption. However, it’s available only in a single 750mg concentration, which may not cater to the dosage needs of all dog sizes and weights.

#### **Verdict**

In terms of pricing, at $0.12 per mg of CBD, Spruce Dog CBD Oil 750mg is positioned at a fair market rate, especially considering its robust cannabinoid profile and recent lab verification.

While the single concentration option could be a limitation for some pet owners, the product’s overall quality and up-to-date lab testing make it a solid choice for those seeking a full-spectrum CBD oil for their dogs.
