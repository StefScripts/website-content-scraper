For dog owners looking for a CBD isolate oil, Medterra’s organic CBD drops for Pets made it our top choice as the ‘best isolate’ in our list of the [best CBD oil for dogs](https://cbdoilfordogs.com/best-cbd-oil-for-dogs/).

Medterra CBD Pet Oil, featuring an isolate CBD extract sourced from organic hemp, presents a specific cannabinoid profile. The latest lab report for their 750mg tincture indicates a total of 793mg of CBD and includes 2.6mg of the additional cannabinoid CBDV.

This single additional cannabinoid is below the average of three found in CBD oils across over 30 brands reviewed. Furthermore, its additional cannabinoid content is effectively 0%, compared to the 8% average.

A positive aspect is the lab report’s date, October 4th, 2023, which is quite recent, ensuring current and reliable product information, a significant improvement over many other products.

Medterra CBD Pet Oil utilizes MCT oil as a carrier, known for its potential health benefits. The product is available in two concentrations, 300mg and 750mg, which may not cover all size preferences for dogs. It is offered in unflavored, beef, or chicken flavors, catering to different canine tastes.

Priced at $0.05 per mg of CBD for the highest concentration and $0.08 for the lowest concentration, the cost is lower than average, reflecting the nature of a CBD isolate product. This pricing is considered fair, aligning with the quality of a CBD isolate product from Medterra.

#### **Verdict**

When evaluating this product, pet owners should consider the recent lab testing, the product’s formulation, and the lower cannabinoid diversity typical of CBD isolates in assessing its overall value and suitability for their pets.
