## Who are Hemp Bombs?

Founded in 2016, Hemp Bombs likes to say that they got into the market “before CBD was cool.” Whether or not you agree with that statement, this Tampa Bay-based brand has established a wide customer base, catering mainly to the recreational CBD market with things like vape juice, gummies, and even a tattoo aftercare balm. Right now, you can find at least some of their products in over 25,000 stores across the country, although only a small proportion of those are likely to stock their pet-specific options.

As you might guess from the branding, Hemp Bombs don’t farm their own plants, which is disappointing. Aside from the actual industrial hemp cultivation, however, Hemp Bombs handles the rest of the production process including the manufacturing of their CBD isolate.

Unlike the vast majority of CBD producers, Hemp Bombs opts for a pure CBD extraction in all of their products. This is good news for tiny dogs or those with sensitive stomachs, though we’re not sure why Hemp Bombs doesn’t also offer a more potent spectrum option. Their pet products are centered around added flavors and soft textures to help pets accept their new CBD schedule.

Although Hemp Bombs only make one type of CBD oil for pets, they offer that oil in several different formulations. There are two natural flavorings, Beef and Chicken, and three concentrations: a 100mg, 250mg, and a 1000mg option. This should help owners achieve the right strength dosage for their dog’s size and overall needs.

For a non-organic isolate extract, each of these strengths offers reasonable value for money, at between 8 and 14 cents per mg of CBD. (Note: this would count as awesome value for money in an organic full-spectrum oil, but Hemp Bomb’s CBD oil is a very different product).

To carry their CBD isolate, Hemp Bombs again breaks the mold, opting for a combination of MCT oil and grapeseed oil. Like the more traditional MCT oil, grapeseed is also a known source of omega-6 fatty acids, which can help maintain organ health when consumed in moderation.

## Our verdict

Hemp Bombs doesn’t come across as a particularly dog-focused company, and the fact that they’re not involved in the farming of their hemp puts them at a disadvantage to many of the other CBD brands we’ve reviewed on this site.
