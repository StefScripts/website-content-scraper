# Discover CBD: CBD Oil for Dogs Brand Review

## About the brand: Who is Discover CBD?

Parent company Discover CBD are the owners of Active CBD, a range of cannabidiol products produced from plants grown in the country’s spiritual home of hemp: Colorado. The brand was founded in Colorado Springs by a small team of three CBD enthusiasts, who realized that their combination of expertise made the perfect portfolio for beginning a CBD business.

With a nutritionist, a hemp Horticulturist, and a medicinal supplement entrepreneur on board, the brand has grown at an impressive rate. Today, Discover CBD has expanded to 5 Colorado storefronts and a bunch of other physical locations across the midwest and east coast.

Discover CBD Active Pet Tincture uses an isolate CBD extract from non-organic hemp. Unfortunately, Discover CBD does not mention where they source their hemp, whereas the best brands use only US grown hemp which has stricter growing regulations.

Looking at its most recent CBD lab report of its 300mg concentration confirms a total of 344mg of CBD, without any additional cannabinoids present. This absence of other cannabinoids means it doesn’t benefit from the entourage effect typically seen in full-spectrum products.

Another downside to Discover CBD’s pet oil is that the most recent lab report available on their website is slightly outdated, dating back to August 25th, 2022. While there are products with older lab reports, the more premium brands’ reports typically fall within a one year range.

The tincture incorporates MCT oil as its carrier oil and is available in bacon or chicken flavors. However, its limited size options of 120mg and 300mg may not suit all dog sizes, particularly those requiring higher CBD doses.

#### **Verdict**

In terms of value, the pricing at $0.09 per mg for the higher concentration and $0.20 per mg for the lower concentration seems steep, especially given the product’s isolate nature and lack of additional beneficial cannabinoids.

This pricing positions Discover CBD Active Pet Tincture at a higher bracket compared to similar quality CBD isolate oils in the market. For those seeking a straightforward, pure CBD product, there are better isolate options in terms of both quality and value.

Medterra’s CBD Pet Oil, for example, is made using organic hemp for roughly half the price. Another alternative is going for a broad spectrum product, like Holistapet’s CBD Oil for Dogs which is packed with other supportive cannabinoids, but similar to isolate products they contain no THC.
