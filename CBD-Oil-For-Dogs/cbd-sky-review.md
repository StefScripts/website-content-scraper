## About the brand: Who is SkyCBD?

CBD Sky is an international CBD brand that does lots of things well, but isn’t quite the best at anything in particular, meaning that they didn’t make it onto our list of the [top 10 CBD oils for dogs](https://cbdoilfordogs.com/best-cbd-oil-for-dogs/). This inclusion of four additional cannabinoids is above the average of three found across CBD oils from over 30 brands reviewed, but the 2% additional cannabinoid content falls short of the 8% average.

For pet owners evaluating this product, the cost, the need for updated quality assurance, and the cannabinoid profile are key factors in assessing its overall value and suitability for their pets.
