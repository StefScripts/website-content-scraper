# HempMy Pet CBD Review

## About the brand: Who is HempMy Pet?

HempMy Pet’s product range has something for every dog, with several different size options and a choice of hemp seed, coconut, and olive oil carrier. Their full spectrum CBD extract is rich in CBG and CBC, two cannabinoids which have been shown to help with canine cancer and anxiety in dogs. Where this brand falls short is in customer testimonials, with no third-party reviews on Google or social media. Also to note, unlike many competitors, HempMy Pet does not provide a money-back guarantee unless the package remains unopened, which might be a consideration for potential buyers.

## Product Reviews

### HempMy Pet Hemp Extracts Oil Review:

  * __

Full spectrum extract

[Shop Now](https://www.hempmypet.com/product/hemp-seed-cbd-oil-for-dogs-1000mg/)

HempMy Pet Hemp Extracts feature a full spectrum CBD extract from organic hemp grown in Colorado. The latest lab report for their 1000mg tincture indicates a CBD content of 981mg, along with 105.8mg of additional cannabinoids, including CBC, CBDa, CBDV, CBG, and THC.

This variety of five additional cannabinoids exceeds the average of three found in CBD oils from over 30 brands reviewed, and the 11% additional cannabinoid content is higher than the 8% average, suggesting a diverse and potent cannabinoid profile.

The lab report, dated July 20, 2022, is relatively recent, though a more current lab analysis would provide better assurance of the product’s current consistency and quality.

HempMy Pet Hemp Extracts utilizes hemp seed oil as a carrier, known for its nutritional benefits. However, the product is available in only two sizes — 250 mg and 1000 mg — which may not cater to all dog sizes and dosing preferences. The tincture is offered without any added flavors, catering to those preferring a natural product.

Regarding pricing, at $0.10 per mg of CBD for the highest concentration and $0.20 for the lowest concentration, the cost is considered to fairly reflect the quality of CBD in HempMy Pet Hemp Extracts.

#### Verdict

However, potential buyers should still consider the availability of more current lab testing and the limited size options when assessing the product’s overall value and suitability for their pets.

### HempMy Pet Hemp Dog Biscuits Review:

  * __

US grown hemp

  * __

Natural ingredients

  * __

HempMy Pet Pumpkin Harvest Organic Hemp Dog Biscuits, like their pet oil, utilize organic hemp sourced from Colorado. Unlike the brand’s CBD oil, their treats use a more processed isolate CBD. The most recent lab report for their 75mg treat pack shows a total of 95 mg of CBD, but it contains no additional cannabinoids.

This lack of additional cannabinoids is below the average of 1.5 found in CBD dog treats across over 30 brands reviewed, and the 0% additional cannabinoid content is considerably lower than the 7% average.

The lab report, dated January 16th, 2023, is relatively recent, which is more current than many other products on the market.

The dog biscuits are made with all-organic ingredients, including pumpkin, garbanzo bean flour, rice flour, tapioca starch, apples, eggs, unrefined coconut oil infused with hemp extract, mint, parsley, and baking powder. The pack contains 15 chews, with each chew providing 5mg of CBD, and they are pumpkin-flavored, likely appealing to dogs.

#### **Verdict**

However, priced at $0.43 per mg of CBD, the cost is considered significantly high compared to the quality of CBD oil provided by HempMy Pet Pumpkin Harvest Organic Hemp Dog Biscuits. This pricing is notably above the market average, especially considering the absence of additional cannabinoids. Potential buyers should consider these factors, particularly the price and cannabinoid content, when evaluating this product for their pets.
