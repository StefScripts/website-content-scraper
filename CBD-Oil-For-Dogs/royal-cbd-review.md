# RoyalCBD for Dogs Review

## About the brand: Royal CBD?

Based in Anaheim California, Royal CBD aren’t hemp growers or cannabis producers. Instead, they’re one of a number of brands who pride themselves on sourcing the best ingredients from producers across the country.

Royal CBD claims to only use fully organic, natural, GMO-free industrial hemp that’s “cultivated without the use of pesticides or other dangerous compounds.” This is great news, but there are no signs that the brand’s pet products carry any official organic certification.

## Product Reviews

### Royal CBD Full Spectrum CBD Pet Oil Review:

  * __

Cannabinoid-rich full spectrum CBD

  * __

Sourced from organically grown US hemp

  * __

Inconvenient sizes (250 and 500mg options)

  * __

Higher price than the quality justifies




[Shop Now](https://royalcbd.com/product/full-spectrum-cbd-pet-oil/)

Royal CBD Full Spectrum CBD Pet Oil, crafted from organic U.S. hemp, offers a robust cannabinoid profile. The latest lab report, dated May 10, 2023, reveals a CBD concentration of 485mg in its 500mg tincture, accompanied by 62mg of supportive cannabinoids from CBC, CBDV, CBG, CBN, and THC.

This diverse mix of 5 additional cannabinoids exceeds the average of 3 found in the CBD oils we reviewed from over 30 brands. Its combined 62mg from the 5 make add 12% to the total cannabinoid content, also higher than the average 8%.

It uses a hemp seed oil as its carrier oil, known to be nutritious but also comes with a stronger taste that does not sit well with all dogs’ taste buds or stomachs. Royal does add bacon flavoring, however, to help mask the stronger carrier oil taste.

Available in just two concentrations – 250mg and 500mg – Royal’s CBD Pet Oil may not be suitable for dogs of all sizes. This is fairly common practice among brands that primarily focus on their human-line of CBD oil.

#### **Verdict**

Despite its quality, the pricing is a consideration. At $0.18 per mg for the 500mg option and $0.24 per mg for the 250mg version, Royal CBD’s oil sits at the higher end of the market. While it’s certainly a premium choice, the cost is somewhat steep compared to other high-quality CBD oils offering similar benefits.

While Royal CBD Full Spectrum CBD Pet Oil is a top-tier product with an impressive cannabinoid range and recent lab verification, its high price point does open the door to lower-priced competitors offering equally good or even better CBD oils.

[Shop Now](https://royalcbd.com/product/cbd-dog-treats/)

Royal Paws CBD Active Treats is the best one from Royal CBD’s pet treat line. Looking at the lab report of its 5 mg treat reveals a total of 6 mg of CBD, as well a combined total of 0.9 mg of additional cannabinoids from CBDa, CBDV, and CBG. Each pack advertises 150mg of CBD with 30 treats that are pumpkin spice cinnamon flavored.

With this presence of 3 additional cannabinoids, they surpass the average of 1.5 found across the 50 CBD dog treats we reviewed, along with an impressive additional cannabinoid content of 18% that’s well above the 7% average. Although the lab report from October 31st, 2022 is relatively recent, an update would bolster confidence in the product’s consistency.

Beyond CBD, Royal Paws enriches these treats with wholesome ingredients such as pumpkin puree, brown rice flour, molasses, cinnamon, hemp seed, and coconut oil. The inclusion of turmeric root and Boswellia adds to their therapeutic potential.

#### **Verdict**

As with more premium products, these Active Treats do come at a higher cost. At $0.33 per mg of CBD, they are nearly double the market average $0.18 per mg of CBD.

### Royal Paws CBD Hearty Treats Review:

  * __

Full spectrum hemp extract

  * __

Made with heart-healthy ingredients

  * __

Slightly outdated CBD lab report (Oct 2022)

  * __

Just one size (150mg)




[Shop Now](https://royalcbd.com/product/cbd-dog-treats/)

Royal Paws CBD Hearty Treats are unique in that they include ingredients high in omega fatty acids that are good for the heart. Due to trace levels of THC, it is also the only full spectrum product in Royal’s CBD dog treat line.

Each of the sweet potato and blueberry flavored treats come in packs of 30 and contain 6.1 mg of CBD, slightly higher than the advertised 5mg, and with a combined 0.3 mg of CBG and THC.

These two additional cannabinoids, like the Calming Treats, are slightly above the average 1.5, but the 0.3mg of CBG and THC make up an extra 6% of cannabinoid content which is slightly below the average of 7%. As with both of Royal’s other dog treats, the latest CBD lab report available was conducted on October 31st, 2022.

They are made with natural ingredients that include sweet potatoes, rolled oats, rice flour, molasses, cinnamon, coconut oil, flax seed, hemp seeds, and blueberries, ensuring a nutritious snack for dogs.

#### **Verdict**

Priced at $0.33 per mg of CBD, the cost is once again on the higher side relative to other CBD dog treats. Due to their unique, heart supportive ingredients, Royal Paws CBD Hearty Treats are a solid choice if you can handle the higher price.
