## About the brand: Who is 4 Corners Cannabis?

4 Corners Cannabis prides itself in offering a farm-to-bottle CBD made with all organic ingredients for both human and pets. It sells both a THC-free and full-spectrum CBD oil for dogs ranging from 250 to 2000mg CBD concentrations.

One aspect that separates 4 Corners is the company’s extraction method, which uses organic sugar cane ethanol to extract the CBD from its Colorado grown hemp plants. This helps preserve more of the cannabinoids and terpenes that provide greater benefits than CBD alone. The downside of this extraction method is a stronger hemp flavor, but 4 Corners does offer a peanut butter flavored oil for dogs sensitive to the hemp taste.
