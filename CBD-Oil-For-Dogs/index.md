## Dosage Guide

All dogs respond to CBD differently – and with so many shapes, sizes, and breeds out there – finding the correct dosage can be tricky.

While CBD is a safe supplement with a low toxicity profile, it is advised to start with a low dose of 0.25 to 0.5 mg CBD per pound of your dogs bodyweight, gradually increasing to a maximum of 4 mg per pound if needed.
