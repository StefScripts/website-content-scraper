# Green Roads CBD for Dogs review

## About the brand: Who is Green Roads?

Green Roads is one of the largest CBD companies in the US, though it mostly focuses on the human cannabis market. They develop a range of traditional and more unusual products, such as CBD edibles and bath bombs. The brand was founded in 2013 by Laura Fuentes, who had been a licensed pharmacist for over two decades before turning to CBD in order to help a close family friend with their health issues.

Since then, Green Roads has grown into the largest privately-owned CBD company in the US (according to the Brightfield Research Group), with their CBD products sold in over 10,000 retail locations across the country—plus a line of CBD oils developed specifically for pets.

Green Roads Dog CBD Pet Drops, utilizing a CBD isolate extracted from non-organic US hemp, offers a straightforward CBD experience. The latest lab analysis shows their 210mg tincture surpassing its advertised potency with a total of 242mg of CBD, yet it notably lacks additional cannabinoids.

This absence of other cannabinoids places the product below the average of three seen in CBD oils across our review of over 30 brands, missing out on the potential benefits of a broader cannabinoid profile.

The lab report’s recent date, September 21st, 2023, ensures current and relevant product data, a crucial aspect for consumers seeking trustworthy information.

Green Roads opts for a blend of MCT and hemp seed oils as carriers, enhancing the product’s bioavailability. The pet drops are available in three tailored sizes: 60, 210, and 600mg of CBD, making it adaptable for various pet sizes and needs. However, these drops are unflavored, catering to pets preferring a natural taste.

#### **Verdict**

While the price point of $0.10 per mg for the largest concentration is somewhat competitive, the smaller 60mg option comes at a steep $0.33 per mg of CBD. Given the product’s reliance on CBD isolate and absence of additional beneficial cannabinoids, such pricing seems excessive compared to the market average.

For pet owners seeking a richer cannabinoid spectrum, exploring alternatives that offer a fuller range of cannabinoids at a more reasonable price would be advisable. Green Roads Dog CBD Pet Drops, while a decent choice for those specifically seeking CBD isolates, may not provide the best value for those desiring a comprehensive cannabinoid profile for their pets.
