#### **What size CBD oil should I buy?**

Many CBD pet brands provide a range of product sizes to ensure you find the most cost-effective option for your dog’s specific needs. Given that CBD dosages are determined by weight, larger dogs typically require significantly more CBD oil than their smaller counterparts.

Dogs experiencing persistent anxiety or stress-related conditions might need higher daily doses compared to dogs dealing with occasional anxieties, such as fear of loud noises, who may only need CBD sporadically.

For owners of larger breeds or dogs with continuous anxiety challenges, opting for CBD oils with a higher concentration can be wise. Although these products come with a higher upfront cost, they often result in more savings over time due to a lower price per mg of CBD, making them a smart choice for long-term use.

#### **Choosing between CBD oil and CBD dog treats for anxiety**  


We typically suggest CBD oils over treats for a couple of key reasons.

Firstly, when CBD is taken with high-fat meals, it can enhance the body’s absorption rate by up to fourfold, which is crucial for managing anxiety more effectively.

Secondly, CBD oils offer more flexibility compared to treats. Pet owners can precisely measure the amount of CBD their dog needs, mixing it into their dog’s preferred meals, snacks, or administering it directly.

However, CBD dog treats have their advantages, especially for anxious pups. Many pet parents appreciate the ease of using pre-prepared treats, and the direct infusion of CBD into these snacks can minimize the potent taste of the hemp plant, making it more palatable for your dog.

#### **What’s the difference between full-spectrum, broad-spectrum, and isolate CBD oil for anxious dogs?**

When considering CBD oil for your anxious dog, you’ll come across terms like full-spectrum, broad-spectrum, and isolate. Here’s what they mean:

  1. **Full-spectrum CBD** is the least processed form, containing CBD, a trace amount of THC, and other cannabinoids found in the hemp plant. This type is recommended because it offers the complete benefits of the hemp plant.
  2. **Broad-spectrum CBD** has been slightly more processed to remove THC but still includes CBD and at least one other cannabinoid, maintaining some of the plant’s synergy without the THC.
  3. **Isolate CBD** is the most refined form, resulting in a pure CBD powder with no other cannabinoids present.



We advocate for full-spectrum CBD oils since they harness the full power of hemp’s cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids, that synergistically work better together than they do alone.

This phenomenon, known as “the entourage effect”, is similar to how eating a whole orange is more beneficial than just taking a vitamin C tablet. The whole orange provides additional nutrients that enhance the body’s use of vitamin C.

The most consistent results in clinical studies of anxiety reduction and CBD have been studies that are using a full spectrum CBD extract.

CBD brands invest significant effort into developing hemp strains rich in a variety of cannabis compounds. Thus, two full-spectrum CBD products that look alike may have different compositions.

To truly understand what’s in a CBD extract, consult the product’s certificate of analysis from a third-party lab. This document will detail the exact composition of the CBD oil, ensuring you choose the best option for your dog’s anxiety.

Study review: A single dose of cannabidiol (CBD) positively influences measures of stress in dogs during separation and car travel

**

Summary:

  * Researchers gave dogs a medium dose of broad-spectrum CBD oil, and other dogs a placebo oil containing no CBD.
  * They then observed the dogs’ stress hormones, heart rates, and overall behavior while they were left alone in a room for 45 minutes, and taken on a 10-minute car ride.
  * Dogs who were given CBD oil showed fewer stress behaviors, seemed less agitated, and had bigger reductions in the stress hormone cortisol.

#### ____Read the full study summary

This study, published in February 2023 in [Frontiers in Veterinary Science](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fvets.2023.1112604/full) by researchers from Waltham Petcare Science Institute in the UK, tested the effects of CBD for anxious dogs who were left alone for 45 minutes and in a crate during ten-minute car rides.

The researchers gathered 40 dogs of various breeds and ages. There were Labradors, Beagles, and Norfolk Terriers, aged between one and nine years old.

The dogs were evenly split into test and control groups.

Each dog was equipped with a special, pet-safe chest monitor and smart collar, which together recorded biological signs of stress.

The study used a single 4 mg/kg dose of broad-spectrum CBD oil capsules (a medium-quality form of pet CBD oil, with fewer natural active compounds than the oils we recommend).

To test the effects of CBD on dogs experiencing separation anxiety, dogs were left in a familiar room for 45 minutes.

As for testing CBD on dogs during car rides, dogs were put into a crate and driven around for 10 minutes.

Dogs’ stress levels were observed by monitoring their serum cortisol, immunoglobulin A, and glucose levels, as well as their heart rate, heart rate variability, distance traveled, body position, and activity levels. Behaviors were also observed – such as how much they whined, paced, panted, and acted in an agitated manner.

##### Results:

Dogs in both the CBD and placebo groups experienced heightened levels of stress hormones during the car ride, but not when left alone in a room.

When comparing the dogs who were given CBD to the placebo group, the researchers found that dogs who’d been given CBD showed lower levels of the stress hormone cortisol compared to baseline, and were judged to be more calm and relaxed by trained observers. IgA and glucose levels, as well as heart rate and heart rate variability, were similar for both groups.
