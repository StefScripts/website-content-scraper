# CBD for Puppies

#### Hemp CBD oil is safe for dogs of all ages, **including puppies**. But do young dogs actually benefit from taking CBD products?

Pet-safe CBD products have grown in popularity in recent years, as a way to support dogs with inflammatory issues like arthritis and anxiety.

Just like older dogs, puppies can experience inflammation due to injuries, genetic conditions, and stress.

As a natural anti-inflammatory supplement, CBD can help lower inflammation in puppies, reducing associated symptoms like discomfort and anxiety.

**So when is CBD appropriate for puppies, and should owners worry about giving their younger dogs CBD products?**

### Is CBD safe for puppies?

Because CBD is a natural supplement made from non-intoxicating hemp plants, it’s completely **safe for puppies** and dogs of any age.

Unlike many human cannabis products, which are made from potent marijuana plants, pet-safe CBD doesn’t cause a high and contains very low amounts of THC – the cannabis compound famous for its psychoactive effects.

THC content in hemp plants and products is federally limited to 0.3% of the total content. While higher levels of THC can be very harmful to puppies, animal healthcare experts agree that cannabis products containing 0.3% THC or lower are safe for pets to consume and cause **no ill effects**.

On the other hand, puppies should never be given human cannabis products. Even CBD oils produced for humans can contain much larger amounts of THC. 

Likewise, it’s important to buy pet CBD from a known provider that uses traceable hemp grown in the US. This helps to ensure your puppy’s product is made using farming and manufacturing best practices.

### Should puppies take CBD?

Even though it’s a supplement rather than a medication, there should be a good reason for puppies to take CBD.

CBD is an anti-inflammatory compound. That means it’s relevant to any inflammatory or immune issue that dogs may have.

Inflammation in puppies can occur for various reasons. Generally, the cause will be:

  * acute (an injury or illness)
  * chronic (a genetic disorder or disease)
  * or due to stress (anxiety or trauma)



**What does that look like?**

For example, a puppy might experience **teething pain** , get stung by a bee, or experience swelling when recovering from fixing and other veterinary procedures. CBD may help lower pain and discomfort by reducing swelling and redness in these situations.

Alternatively, a puppy may develop **hip dysplasia** and other mobility problems that some breeds are particularly prone to. Here, CBD can help maintain and improve joint function.

For those puppies who develop more serious illness, **gastrointestinal issues** , or seizures. CBD can reduce nausea, convulsions, and other inflammatory symptoms.

Puppies that experience stress due to **separation anxiety** may become calmer and more receptive to training methods when supplementing with CBD.

_If a puppy isn’t experiencing inflammation, there’s no need to supplement CBD._

#### What **shouldn’t** pet CBD be used for?

  * **As a replacement for prescription medications.** Even though clinical studies have shown CBD’s ability to reduce inflammation, it shouldn’t be thought of as a replacement for medications prescribed by vets. In some cases, pets may be able to transition to CBD from an existing treatment, but only under the guidance of an animal healthcare expert.


  * **As an alternative to behavior training.** The soothing effects of CBD can improve dogs’ behavior, as they become less agitated by inflammatory symptoms. But proper socialization and training methods are still crucial for helping to manage dogs’ wider wellbeing, making them feel safe and assured across different situations.


  * **As a sedative.** Like many anti-anxiety supplements and medications, CBD may cause some dogs to feel sleepy with high doses. While this is a safe side effect, owners shouldn’t take advantage of this effect to sedate dogs, but rather let the natural calming effects of CBD help improve your pup’s sleep.



### How to give CBD to a puppy

Administration is easiest by dropping CBD oil into your puppy’s meals. CBD oil is recommended as it is better absorbed than treats, is easier to give more precise doses, and is the more cost-effective option.

Even though there’s no real risk of overdose from pet-safe CBD supplements, it’s important to find the right _[CBD dosage](https://cbdoilfordogs.com/cbd-dosage-for-dogs/)_ for your puppy, and not to give them more CBD than necessary.

#### CBD dosage for puppies:

While most brands and experts recommend a starting dosage of 0.25 to 0.5 mg of CBD per pound of a dog’s body weight, we recommend starting on the lower end of 0.25mg of CBD per pound of your puppy’s bodyweight.

So, if your puppy weighs 10 pounds, then 2.5 mg of CBD should be a good initial dose. Remember that CBD extracts are infused into a carrier oil, so owners will need to divide a product’s total volume by the listed concentration to work out the right dose.

Even before this starting dose, many owners will give their dogs a small taste of CBD to ensure they tolerate it well and don’t experience possible side effects such as fatigue or diarrhea.

Dosage schedule  
There are several ways to give CBD to puppies depending upon their needs. Owners can decide whether it’s more appropriate to administer CBD at the point of need or on a consistent daily schedule.

– For example, you might give your dog a CBD dose whenever their exhibit symptoms of teething pain or separation anxiety, up to a maximum of two doses per day.

– Or you might mix a dose of CBD oil into your puppy’s dinner each evening.

Because puppies often have more sensitive stomachs than fully grown dogs, it can be helpful to mix CBD with a good amount of food or to drop CBD onto a treat.

#### Talk to your veterinarian

Any time you decide to start a new course of supplements with your puppy – CBD included – it’s important to consult with your vet.

While vets can’t yet formally prescribe pet CBD products, many are familiar with CBD and will be able to advise owners on whether supplementation is an appropriate step.

### CBD for puppies to sleep

While all dogs need sleep, it particularly important for puppies, as they need their downtime for rest, recovery, and growth.

CBD can improve a puppy’s quality of sleep by helping to calm them from everyday stressors. It shouldn’t act as a sedative, however. If your puppy experiences drowsiness after ingesting CBD, you may be giving them too high of a dose.

A 2019 study looking at the effects of CBD on sleep in human participants [found that 2/3 experienced sleep improvements](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6326553/) when taking CBD products. CBD works similarly across all mammals, suggesting that there is potential for improving sleep in puppies.

### CBD for puppy separation anxiety

One of the most popular uses of CBD oil in dogs of all ages is to help combat separation anxiety.

Canine separation anxiety occurs when dogs form a “hyper attachment” with an owner or another pet in their home. When a dog becomes separated from its attached partner, they react with panic and agitation.

Separation anxiety reactions can occur even if a dog is apart from its attached person for even short periods of time.

The most common way to use CBD for separation anxiety in puppies is to combine a daily dosage of CBD with behavioral training methods such as crate training and desensitization.
