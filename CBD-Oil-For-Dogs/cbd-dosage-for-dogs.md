# CBD Dosage for Dogs: How much CBD oil to give your dog

How much CBD oil to give your dog is dependent on (1) their weight, (2) the specific ailment being treated, and (3) their experience with CBD.

Most published CBD for dog studies have used dosages around 1 mg of CBD per pound, but we **recommend a starting dose of 0.25 to 0.5 mg** of CBD per pound of your dog’s weight, and increasing from their if needed. While your dog’s dosage may need to gradually increase over time, some dog owners have seen major improvements even with these lower doses.

  * **start with 0.25 – 0.5 mg of CBD per pound** of your dogs body weight
  * **gradually increase the dose** if needed, up to a maximum dose of **4 mg / pound**

### Can I give my dog too much CBD?

Overdosing is extremely rare (if not impossible) with CBD for dogs. A 2018 study examining CBD safety for dogs showed no cases of adverse effects when 8 mg/kg CBD (roughly 4 mg per pound) was administered.

*While overdosing is almost impossible, always monitor you pup’s behavior when administering a new supplement or medication, and consult with a veterinarian if any adverse reactions do occur.

### How often should I give my dog CBD?

Frequency of CBD oil administration may vary based on the specific issue you’re addressing and your dog’s individual response to the supplement. Here are some general suggestions:

  * __

_For anxiety_ : **daily or on an as-needed basis** , depending on the severity and form of your dog’s anxiety. If your dog experiences situational anxiety (e.g., from loud noises or unfamiliar situations), you can administer CBD oil 30-60 minutes before the anticipated event. For dogs with general anxiety, daily administration at the same time each day might be more effective.

  * __

For pain-relief & arthritis: **daily or twice per day** for dogs suffering chronic pain, such as arthritis or joint pain. You should adjust the dosage or frequency based on your dog’s response and the severity of their pain. For acute pain, such as post-surgery or injury, you can administer CBD oil on an as-needed basis, following your veterinarian’s guidance.

  * __

_For seizures_ : **once or twice per day,** depending on the severity and frequency of your dog’s seizures, as well as their individual response to the supplement. In a 2018 study that saw an 89% reduction in seizures during the trial, dogs were given CBD twice per day over the course of 4 weeks.




### Other CBD dosage factors to consider

**CBD quality:** It’s important to use a high-quality CBD oil from a reputable source that has been tested by third-party labs to ensure purity and potency. Choose a CBD oil specifically formulated for pets, as some human CBD products may contain ingredients that could be harmful to animals.

**CBD accuracy:** As hemp is a naturally grown product, every batch of CBD will vary slightly in terms of cannabinoid structure and actual levels of CBD. However, brands that source their CBD from more reputable farms that use better extraction processes will be more consistent.

For example, while Charlotte’s Web is a popular brand, we found that the CBD reported on its Hemp Extract for Dogs label only reflected the amount of CBD reported by its lab results by CBD by 85%, making precise and consistent dosing more difficult. Brands like Honest Paws and Penelope’s Bloom, however, reported accurate CBD levels by 97%.

**CBD oil vs CBD dog treats:** There can be a difference in how much CBD is absorbed by dogs when giving them CBD oil versus a CBD treat. Higher fat content in food can help with the absorption of CBD, so we recommend CBD oils for dogs over CBD dog treats. CBD is lipophilic, meaning it dissolves more readily in fats and oils.

  * CBD oil is typically administered sublingually (under the tongue) or mixed with food. When given sublingually, the CBD is absorbed directly into the bloodstream through the blood vessels under the tongue. This method allows for faster and more efficient absorption compared to CBD treats, which need to pass through the digestive system before being absorbed.
  * CBD treats, on the other hand, undergo a process called “first-pass metabolism” when ingested. This means the CBD must pass through the liver before entering the bloodstream, which can lead to a lower bioavailability (the amount of CBD that reaches the bloodstream and is available for use by the body). However, CBD treats are also effective, and can be a more convenient or complimentary option to CBD oil.

### Summary

1\. **Gradual introduction:** Start with a low dose between 0.25 to 0.5 mg of CBD per pound and slowly increase it over time, while closely monitoring your dog’s response. This will allow you to gauge their tolerance and find the most effective dosage for their specific needs. If you notice any adverse side effects, discontinue use and consult with your veterinarian.

2\. **Consistency:** Administer the CBD oil consistently, using oils or treats from a reputable brand that regularly tests its products. If you can, try to administer CBD to your pup at the same time each day. This will help your dog’s body adjust to the new treatment and may make it more effective over time.

3\. **Monitor and adjust:** Keep a close eye on your dog as you introduce CBD oil and adjust their dosage as needed. It may take some time to find the right dose for your pet, so be patient and don’t hesitate to consult with your veterinarian if you have any concerns or questions.
