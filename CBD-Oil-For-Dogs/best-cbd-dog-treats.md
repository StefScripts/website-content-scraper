#### How to give your dog CBD treats

Because CBD treats contain active health supplements, owners should limit how many treats their dogs consume per day. Make sure to stick to a dog’s recommended dose as well as any instructions on the packaging.

Aside from quantity, feel free to give your dog CBD treats in all the same ways you would normally use treats and chews — as rewards, on walks, or whenever they need a tasty snack.

##### CBD dog treats: Dosage instructions

How much CBD your dog should have per day depends upon 3 things: the strength of treats, the size of the dog, and the severity of the dog’s symptoms.

A good starting point for most dogs is around 0.5 mg of CBD per pound of bodyweight.

As dogs become more accustomed to their supplements, they may need to increase their dosage over time. Likewise, dogs with severe inflammation or anxiety may benefit from higher dosage.

Most experts recommend starting low, going slow, and carefully observing pups for any changing behaviors. It’s always a good idea to read package instructions carefully and to talk to a vet about any questions or concerns.

Here’s our [full guide on canine CBD dosages](https://cbdoilfordogs.com/cbd-dosage-for-dogs/), which has a more detailed look at how to give your dog CBD.

###### How many CBD treats should you give your dog?

Most CBD treats contain between a half and whole dose of hemp extract, so you should be able to give your dog 1-2 treats per day. It’s also fine to split treats into smaller chunks, as long as pups eventually get the entire dose.

#### Are CBD treats safe for dogs?

As long as CBD treats are made by reliable brands using quality ingredients, they are completely safe for dogs. Studies show that CBD is highly compatible and non-toxic for humans and animals.

Even in large doses, pure CBD is unlikely to cause any serious symptoms, and dogs would need to eat many treats to approach levels considered unsafe.

Remember that CBD pet treats should be treated like any other supplement or medication – follow any manufacturer instructions and don’t treat them as a general dog food product.

Pet CBD products should always be made from hemp plants, not marijuana.

##### Can dogs take CBD alongside other medications?

Always consult a vet when considering combining a dog’s course of prescribed medication with supplements such as CBD.

While CBD is generally compatible with other medications, there’s been some evidence that moderate CBD consumption may affect the way that dogs’ bodies metabolize certain drugs.

**Can dogs experience any side effects from taking CBD?**

Compared to many of the drugs and treatments traditionally offered to dogs and their owners, CBD has a minor side effect profile and is generally well tolerated.

Some dogs may be more sensitive to CBD than others and can experience sleepiness, nausea, or ataxia (coordination issues) when trying CBD.

Dogs with highly sensitive stomachs may also experience diarrhea or stomach upset when trying CBD treats for the first time – either from the CBD extract itself or from other parts of the recipe.

#### Are CBD dog treats effective?

Over the last 10 years, pet owners, vets, and researchers have observed the anti-inflammatory potential of hemp extracts when given to dogs with issues such as arthritis, seizures, and moderate anxiety.

CBD is most effective for dogs when consistently consumed in moderate quantities over a number of weeks.

Dogs without inflammatory disorders are unlikely to experience any benefits from CBD.

A [study by Cornell University](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fvets.2018.00165/full) on the effects of CBD on dogs with arthritis and joint pain found that a 2 mg / kg (or ~0.5 mg / lbs.) dose every 12 hours resulted in “a significant decrease in pain and increase in activity” over the 4-week study.

#### Should I try CBD treats or CBD oil for dogs?

There’s no difference in the type or quality of CBD that goes into oils or treats for dogs.

The main factor in deciding which type of pet CBD product is right for you tends to come down to dosing preferences.

CBD oils come in tinctures with graded droppers so that owners can decide how much CBD to give their dog. Using a dropper allows flexible dosing methods – owners can try adding CBD to food, into different treats, or straight into their pet’s mouth.

CBD treats are pre-dosed and portioned for owners to easily provide their pups with the CBD they need – both at home and on the go.

Instead of a treat recipe, CBD oils mix hemp extracts with fatty oils to aid digestion. Many brands use hemp seed oil, coconut oil, or olive oil to transport their CBD. They also offer different concentrations to suit dogs of different sizes and needs.
