# [Dr. Desiree Prisman](./)

## Services

THERAPY  
  
Dr. Prisman practices Cognitive Behavior Therapy and Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT). She is experienced in treating a wide array of issues and disorders, including:  
  
Depression  
Anxiety Disorders  
Obsessive Compulsive Disorder  
Post Traumatic Stress Disorder  
Eating Disorders  
Borderline Personality Disorder  
Self Harm & Suicidal Behaviors  
Grief & Loss   
Intimacy Issues  
Relationship Difficulties  
Low Self Esteem  
Anger Issues  
Stress Management  
Life Transitions

Dr. Prisman's compassionate, yet firm, approach has helped hundreds of patients lead happier and more productive lives. She believes is helping her patients reach the quality of life they desire.   
  
Dr. Prisman is highly sought after and genuinely cares for every patient.   
  
OTHER SERVICES

Dr. Prisman provides Psychological Assessments and Medical Legal Reports.   
  
SEMINARS & WORKSHOPS:  
  
Dr. Prisman offers seminars and workshops based on her experience in applying Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) in a hospital setting and in private practice. Sessions include the teaching of DBT Principles, Techniques, and Skills Training, as well as supervision. These are available for either 10 session workshops, 1 day seminars, or half-day training.
