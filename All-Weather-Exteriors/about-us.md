### **SOFFIT AND FASCIA WORK – COMBINED WITH ROOFING**

The fascia and [soffit ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soffit)are often overlooked areas of a home’s exterior that play a vital role in protecting the structural integrity of your roof and home. As a leading Winnipeg roofing company, we understand the importance of properly installing and maintaining these components. If the fascia or soffits are not correctly installed or repaired, your home will be significantly more vulnerable to weather damage.

That’s why we recommend addressing the entire exterior of your home at once for the best results. Let our experienced team handle your roofing, siding, fascia, and soffit project from start to finish. We will ensure all areas are properly secured and sealed to withstand Winnipeg’s harsh climate.

The fascia boards run vertically along the ends of the roof’s rafters. In addition to covering the rafters, they provide an anchoring surface for gutters. Fascia boards are usually painted or wrapped in aluminum to maintain an attractive and weather-resistant finish.

Installed beneath the roof’s overhanging eaves, the soffits form a barrier that keeps debris and wildlife out of the attic space. The soffit also allows airflow into the attic, which is crucial for proper ventilation and circulation. Surprisingly, the conditions in your attic can greatly impact the lifespan of your roofing materials above.

Inadequate attic ventilation may also lead to higher heating and cooling bills as hot or cold air gets trapped. We ensure your attic is well-ventilated to regulate indoor temperatures and protect your home’s structural integrity.

Our team provides complete fascia and soffit repair, replacement, and installation. We work with you to select finishes that seamlessly blend with your home’s design aesthetic.
