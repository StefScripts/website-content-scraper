## FAQs

How long does a typical roof replacement take in Winnipeg's climate?

Most residential roof replacements in Winnipeg can be completed in 1-3 days, depending on your roof’s size and complexity. At All Weather Exteriors, we plan installations around Manitoba’s weather patterns and can typically complete most projects even during the shorter construction season. We always ensure proper scheduling to protect your home during the installation process.

How much does a new roof cost in Winnipeg?

The cost of a new roof in Winnipeg typically ranges from **$4500** to **$12500** , depending on factors such as your roof’s size, chosen materials, and complexity. As Winnipeg’s leading roofing contractor, we provide detailed, transparent quotes and flexible financing options to help make your roof replacement more affordable.

What warranties do you offer on roofing installations?

We stand behind our work with comprehensive warranty coverage. Our asphalt shingle installations come with a limited lifetime warranty as standard, which includes full coverage for the first 10 years and then gradually pro-rates at a slow rate, ensuring you maintain a percentage of warranty protection throughout the life of your roof. For homeowners seeking maximum protection, we offer an upgraded warranty option at an additional cost that provides non-prorated lifetime coverage for a full 50 years. This premium warranty option gives you complete peace of mind for decades to come. As Winnipeg’s established roofing company, we’ll be here to honor these warranties long-term, protecting your investment in your home.

Are you licensed and insured for roofing work in Winnipeg?

Yes, All Weather Exteriors is fully licensed, bonded, and insured for all roofing work in Winnipeg. We maintain comprehensive liability insurance and WCB coverage for all our workers. Our team is certified by major manufacturers and follows all Manitoba building codes and regulations.

How can I tell if my roof needs replacement versus repair in Winnipeg?

As experienced Winnipeg roofers, we can help assess your roof’s condition through our comprehensive inspection service. Common signs that indicate you may need a replacement include shingles that are curling, buckling, or missing; granules in your gutters; daylight showing through roof boards; and water stains on your ceiling. We’ll provide an honest assessment – if a repair can safely extend your roof’s life, we’ll recommend that option. However, if a replacement would be more cost-effective in the long run, we’ll explain why.
